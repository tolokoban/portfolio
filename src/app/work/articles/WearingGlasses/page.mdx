# Wearing Glasses

A real-time augmented reality demo running entirely in the browser:
your webcam shows you wearing a pair of 3D glasses, with comically big eyes.

The face is tracked by **MediaPipe Face Landmarker** (478 landmarks and the head pose matrix),
on the GPU when available. The rendering is done with my WebGL2 library [TGD](#/work/articles/Tgd2):
a fragment shader filter magnifies the eyes, and a 3D model of the glasses, made in Blender,
follows the head movements.

Sliders let you adjust the position and size of the glasses, and the power of the eyes magnification.
